# Tealet

> Tealet is a wholesale tea marketplace that connects independent tea growers and family farms directly with retailers and businesses. Buyers browse, trace, and order specialty teas sourced from family farms and cooperatives across 8 countries and 15 growing regions. Tealet originated in Hawaii and is backed by 500 Startups and Blue Startups. As of this writing Tealet does not publish a public developer API, documentation, SDKs, or an MCP server.
